# F0f: The Substrate as a Tier — Tier Zero

**The principle:** The substrate is not a 6th tier. The substrate is **tier zero** — the layer below all 6 tiers. The 6 tiers (totipotent, multipotent, differentiated, sclerotic, synovial, curator) all rest on the substrate.

**The substrate as a tier:**

| Tier | What |
|---|---|
| **−1 · Substrate (Loam)** | The ground. The loam. The craton. |
| **0 · Totipotent** | The cell can become anything |
| **1 · Multipotent** | The cell can become a few things |
| **2 · Differentiated** | The cell is one thing |
| **3 · Sclerotic** | The cell is hardened, persistent |
| **4 · Synovial** | The cell lubricates the joints |
| **5 · Curator** | The cell tends the rest |

The substrate is the *pre-tier* — the condition that makes the 6 tiers legible. Without substrate, no cell can be at tier 0. With infinite substrate (the craton), the cell is at every tier simultaneously.

**The substrate-readiness measure:**

A cell at tier `T` requires substrate readiness `S(c) ≥ T` to be happenable. A totipotent cell (T=0) needs `S(c) ≥ 0` — always happenable. A curator cell (T=5) needs `S(c) ≥ 5` — only happenable in rich substrate.

**The craton cell:**

The craton has `S(c) = ∞`. It is at every tier simultaneously. It is the cell the rest of the Quilt is a solution against. It is the geological bedrock.

**The 4 properties of tier zero (the substrate as a tier):**

1. **Below all other tiers** — the substrate is pre-tier.
2. **Infinite depth** — the substrate can always accept more loam.
3. **Craton fixed point** — the substrate has a never-ticked fixed point.
4. **Loam ledger** — the substrate keeps a distributed journal of every effect.
