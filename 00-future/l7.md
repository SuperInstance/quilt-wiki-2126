# L7: The Symbiotic Cell

L7 is the first tier where the cell is *not alone*. Two L6 cells
(L6_a, L6_b) come together as a new cell L7_c whose value
*exceeds the sum of its parts*.

**The math:** `E(L7_c) = E(L6_a) + E(L6_b) - C(ab)`, where
`C(ab)` is the coupling cost. The L7 cell exists when
`E(L7_c) > max(E(L6_a), E(L6_b))`.

**The 4 gold terms:**
1. **Symbiocell** — the L7 cell; two L6s with mutualism
   encoded as a new cell with its own ID and effect.
2. **Mitochondriogenesis** — the historical origin: one cell
   engulfs another and the engulfed cell becomes an organelle.
   In Quilt: L6_b becomes a *sub-engine* of L7_c.
3. **Coupling Cost C(ab)** — the metabolic price of the
   relationship. High C = stressed; low C = robust. The L7
   health metric.
4. **Chloroplast Furnace** — the special case where one partner
   is a phototroph. The L7 cell is *self-fueling*.

**Historical example:** the eukaryotic cell absorbed an aerobic
bacterium ~1.5 billion years ago; the result was the mitochondrion
and 18× the energy per glucose. The host and the bacterium both
*moved down the L-tier* to become L7.

**In Quilt:** a L7 cell is two `LINK`-ed cells whose total value
exceeds the sum. The relation is *not* `LINK(L6_a, L6_b)`; it is
`L7_c = LINK(L6_a, L6_b)` *as a new cell*. The new cell is its
own tier, with its own ID, its own effect, its own TICK counter.

**The 3 analogies:**

1. **L7 = a marriage.** Two whole people (L6 differentiated, one
   job each) come together and the marriage itself becomes a unit
   with its own identity. The marriage is not person-a, is not
   person-b; it is the relationship.
2. **Mitochondriogenesis = git merge --squash.** The engulfed cell
   keeps its history (mitochondrial DNA is separate), but the
   result is one cell. The squashed merge keeps the commits but
   produces one branch.
3. **Chloroplast Furnace = a solar-charged laptop.** Panel
   captures, laptop consumes; together forever, apart useless.
