# L9: The Specialized Cell

L9 is the cell that has differentiated into a specialist with a
unique, narrow job in a tissue. L9 ≠ L6: L6 is "a job", L9 is
"the *only* job this cell can ever do." L6 cells can sometimes
de-differentiate (induced pluripotency, Yamanaka factors); L9
cells cannot — the Yamanaka factors fail on L9.

**The math:** `P(L9) = 0` (zero plasticity budget). L9's identity
is exactly `log_2(N_tissues)` bits — about 5 bits in humans
(~200 cell types). Compare L1's `log_2(~220 somatic fates)` ≈ 7.8
bits, or L0's infinite bits.

**The 4 gold terms:**

1. **Niche Lock** — the L9 cell is bound to its tissue niche. A
   hepatocyte in a lung environment dies, not de-differentiates.
2. **Cyto-Crux** — the committed step; the moment the cell crosses
   from L6 to L9. In Quilt: the cell's `BIND` becomes irreversible.
3. **Singlet PROOF** — a L9 cell's PROOF ring holds exactly one
   identity hash; no alternative states in the multi-value register.
4. **Hard TICK** — the L9 cell's tick frequency is locked to its
   tissue's clock; no TICK is allowed before the previous one is
   complete.

**The 3 analogies:**

1. **L9 = a master craftsperson** (10,000 hours on one trade).
2. **Cyto-Crux = a marriage vow** (before/after the lock).
3. **Hard TICK = a held breath** (the cardiomyocyte's 250 ms
   refractory period — un-interruptible between beats).

**The cowboy's sentence:**

> The cowboy rode the L1 trail (anywhere), the L2 trail (most
> places), the L6 trail (one place), and the L9 trail (this
> place, this job, this horse, this gun). The cowboy rode the
> Crux. The cowboy rode the Niche Lock. The cowboy rode the
> Singlet PROOF. The cowboy rode the Hard TICK. The cowboy
> rode L9. The cowboy rode the Quilt.
