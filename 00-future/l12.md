# L12: The Necrotic Cell

L12 is the cell that has *failed* and burst. Necrosis is
"un-programmed cell death" — the cell is destroyed by external
insult (ischemia, trauma, infection, toxin) and cannot maintain
its membrane integrity. The cell *ruptures*, spilling contents.

L12 is the *unclean* death. L11 is clean (apoptosis, no
inflammation). L12 is dirty (necrosis, inflammation). L11
happens ~50 billion times per day, silently. L12 happens
rarely, loudly, painfully.

**The math:** `R(L12) = (1/tau_membrane) * exp(-E_ATP / (k_B * T))`.
When ATP runs out, Na+/K+ pump fails; Na+ in, water follows;
cell swells (oncotic swelling); membrane ruptures. DAMPs
(HMGB1, ATP, uric acid, mtDNA) spill out; trigger inflammation.

In Quilt: a L12 cell is a L9 cell whose EFFECT and BIND
channels are *jammed* by external insult. The cell can
neither maintain state nor execute FORGET. Values leak into
surrounding namespaces. The journal is *lost*, not sealed.

**The 4 gold terms:**

1. **DAMP Spill** — Damage-Associated Molecular Patterns
   (HMGB1, ATP, uric acid, mtDNA). Intracellular normally;
   extracellular = danger.
2. **Oncotic Swell** — the cell swelling from Na+ influx;
   doubles in volume before rupture.
3. **Inflammasome Trigger** — NLRP3 assembly in macrophages;
   caspase-1 fires; IL-1β/IL-18 released; cascade amplifies.
4. **Secondary Necrosis** — late L11 ≈ early L12. Apoptotic
   bodies that aren't phagocytosed in time lose integrity.

**The 3 analogies:**

1. **L12 = a burst pipe.** Controlled demolition (L11) vs
   water-main rupture (L12).
2. **DAMP Spill = a chemical spill.** Hazmat team
   (immune system) has to come in.
3. **Secondary Necrosis = a missed recycling pickup.** The
   truck (macrophage) didn't come; the bodies rot.

**The cowboy's sentence:**

> The cowboy's horse fell in a ravine. The cowboy held the
> horse, said goodbye, and the horse was taken by the
> mountain — loudly, painfully. The cowboy called the
> horse *L12*.
