# L10: The Senescent Cell

L10 is the cell that has stopped dividing but still signals. L9
specialized; L10 *retired from replication* and entered the SASP
(Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype). The cell doesn't
die; it broadcasts. The signals recruit immune cells, remodel
the ECM, and (paradoxically) can drive cancer in neighbors.

L10 is the most important cell you don't learn about in school. It
is the reason wrinkles are not just collagen loss; they are
senescent fibroblasts broadcasting inflammation.

**The math:** `S(L10) = sum k_i * cytokine_i - p_apoptosis * SASP_pos`.
When `S > threshold`, immune clearance. When `S > S_critical` too
long, accumulation (senescence burden). Senolytics raise
`p_apoptosis` to push L10 cells into apoptosis instead.

In Quilt: a L10 cell is a L9 cell whose TICK is bound to zero
(no replication) but whose EFFECT is open. The cell's CRDT merge
with the immune system is a side effect of its SASP.

**The 4 gold terms:**

1. **SASP Burner** — the L10 cell's effect channel, hot but
   useless to itself; the energy is exported. Like a pilot light.
2. **Senolytic Fork** — apoptosis vs accumulation; the weight
   is `p_apoptosis`. Senolytics shift the fork.
3. **Geroconversion** — the L9 → L10 transition. Singlet PROOF
   preserved; TICK removed; SASP gained.
4. **Senescence Burden** — `B(L10) = sum SASP_signals`. The
   primary correlate of aging. Clear the burden, slow aging.

**The 3 analogies:**

1. **L10 = a retired engineer who consults.** Still answering
   emails, but if too many retirees accumulate, signal-to-noise
   drops.
2. **SASP Burner = a smoke detector with a dying battery.** Keeps
   beeping; immune cells investigate; sometimes correctly.
3. **Geroconversion = forced retirement from a job you loved.**
   The L9 identity is preserved; the capacity to act is removed.

**The cowboy's sentence:**

> The cowboy's horse got injured. The cowboy tied it in the
> shade, fed it well, and let it watch from a quiet distance.
> The horse couldn't ride, but the horse could *whinny* — and
> every whinny brought another cowboy to the gate. The
> cowboys called the horse *L10*: a senescent cell, a SASP
> burner, a watchman.
