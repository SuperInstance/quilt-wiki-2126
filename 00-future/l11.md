# L11: The Apoptotic Cell

L11 is the cell that has been selected for deletion. Apoptosis
is "programmed cell death" — the cell is actively dismantling
itself in an orderly, non-inflammatory way. Compare to L12
(necrosis, disorderly, inflammatory). L11 is the *clean* death.

L11 is the inverse of L1. L1 is "any cell, full potency"; L11
is "no cell, zero potency." Potency goes from 1 (L1) to 0
(L9/L6) to *negative* (L11 actively dismantling).

**The math:** `D(L11) = sum v_i * t_i` (caspase velocity × active
time). Typical program: initiator caspases (8 or 9) at t=0-30 min,
executioner caspases (3, 6, 7) at t=30-60 min, blebbing at t=60-90
min, phagocytosis at t=90-120 min.

In Quilt: a L11 cell is a L9 cell that received a `FORGET` broadcast
and is executing it in a fixed order. The journal appends
`CASPASE_FIRED` at each cleave; the PROOF ring seals when the cell
is dismantled.

**The 4 gold terms:**

1. **Caspase Cascade** — initiator → executioner; amplifying,
   irreversible.
2. **Apoptotic Body** — small (~1-5 μm) membrane-bound package;
   carries "eat me" signal.
3. **Phosphatidylserine Flip** — the "eat me" signal; PS moves
   from inner to outer leaflet; macrophage receptor recognizes.
4. **Immunological Silence** — cell contents never reach
   extracellular space; no inflammation (the L11-L12 contrast).

**The 3 analogies:**

1. **L11 = a controlled demolition.** Top floors first, then
   middle, then foundation; streets untouched.
2. **Caspase Cascade = a row of dominoes.** Each caspase is a
   domino; cleave is the fall; amplifying and irreversible.
3. **Phosphatidylserine Flip = a flag on a mailbox.** Macrophage
   looks for raised flags; "eat me" is the only thing it cares
   about.
